This explainer follows Traits’ core lifecycle: a user correction becomes a
drafted, compared, explicitly approved Markdown trait, which then guides review,
correction, and rechecking of a later edit. It draws its purpose from the root
README’s opening overview, its checkout context from **Start**, and its flow and
invariants from **From feedback to habit**, including the Mermaid diagram and
the two-tool summary; the seven tabs in `traits.sh/src/LifecycleStep.ts` and
their visuals supply the concrete case-guard example. It follows a new, approved
preference and a confirmed applicable finding, skipping setup, covered-rule
reuse, clarification, conflict reconciliation, and other decision branches.
`scene.md` is the complete 75-second scene brief, and `narration/script.json`
contains the nine spoken clips and their exact visual anchors; these are source
artifacts for a Psychopomp Scene Program, not a rendered film.
