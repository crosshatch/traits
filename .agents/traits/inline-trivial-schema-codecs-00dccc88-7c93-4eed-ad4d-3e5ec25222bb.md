Inline trivial schema/codec expressions, such as S.fromJsonString(S.JsonObject), and one-expression helper wrappers at their use sites when they remain short and readable, even when repeated. Keep named definitions for domain concepts, substantial reusable logic, improved readability, or meaningful measured construction cost.
