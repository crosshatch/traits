Prefer equivalent utilities from Effect’s modules over JavaScript built-in methods and functions, including Array, Record, BigInt, Boolean, Number, RegExp, String, and Struct: use `Array.map(items, f)` instead of `items.map(f)`. Use built-ins when no suitable Effect equivalent exists or when required to preserve behavior. For local mutable accumulators, prefer built-in mutations when Effect has no equivalent mutation: `items.push(value)`, not copying and reassigning with `items = Array.append(items, value)`.
